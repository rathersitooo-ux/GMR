# Battle SFX source and license record

All original clips below come from Kenney's CC0 packs. They are distributed under Creative Commons CC0 1.0 and require no attribution. The tracked files are mono 44.1 kHz MP3 transcodes at 64 kbit/s for compact, broadly decodable Web Audio buffers. Clip IDs preserve the original pack filename and suffix.

Each cue selects a source clip and a small playback-rate variation from the battle event ID. The mix stays deliberately quiet under the character animation, with a short per-cue duration cap. Audio begins only after a pointer, touch, or keyboard gesture unlocks Web Audio; reduced-motion/low-performance static scenes emit no cues.
